# Decision log

1. Record only decisions supported by source evidence; include date and decision owner when known.
2. Separate decision, rationale, rejected alternatives, constraints, and consequences.
3. Mark assumptions and unresolved questions explicitly.
4. Link to the canonical artifact rather than copying large discussions.
5. Preserve superseded decisions with status instead of silently deleting them.

Do not convert casual suggestions into settled decisions.
